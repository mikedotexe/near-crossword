# Product and boundaries

Sponsors prefund short learning campaigns. Participants read a lesson, solve its
crossword, and can receive a fixed small USDC reward on Base while slots remain.
Optional campaign preparation is paid separately through x402. NEAR AI provides
inference; the operator intends to fund its credits with its own staking yield.

Keep sponsor reward principal, service revenue, inference credits and operator
stake separate. Never stake sponsor principal or subsidize a missing campaign
deposit from a shared balance. Chain receipts prove payments and budget rules,
not unique humans, learning or truthful application eligibility decisions.

AI produces drafts, never eligibility decisions. Human approval binds a revision
and its public terms. Matching source quotations is necessary, not sufficient for
correctness. Public lessons contain clues, not answer/evidence material. Email
verification is required at claim; sponsor contact sharing is separately optional.
Contacts, raw answers, wallet proofs and signing material stay out of public logs.

The existing NEAR app/contracts are still production. Preserve their liabilities
and claims; do not reinterpret their encoding as Base typed data. Base Batches
fit is optional. See the [agreed work order](../docs/reshape-action-plan.md) for
scope and the [launch register](../docs/early-launch-status.md) for actual state.
