## Base-first economics

- Base is the native reward and accounting rail.
- Learners receive the entire advertised USDC reward.
- Sponsored gas and Crossword product fees never reduce escrow principal.
- Base campaigns carry no chain-integration surcharge.
- Sponsors pay separately for campaign creation and reporting.
- A future non-Base deployment is a paid custom integration with separate gas
  and settlement infrastructure.

This position is technically grounded. CDP's managed paymaster supports Base and
Base Sepolia, with contract allowlists and spend controls:
https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/paymaster/introduction/welcome
